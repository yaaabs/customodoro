📚 What is the Pomodoro Technique?

Think in tomatoes, not hours. The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method that breaks your workday into focused 25-minute sessions, followed by refreshing breaks. Created by Francesco Cirillo, this approach helps many people structure focus time and recovery time.

What is Customodoro?

A free, customizable Pomodoro timer with Classic countdowns and Reverse Pomodoro (Flowmodoro) sessions. Start instantly, shape your focus routine, and keep your progress with or without an account.

Explore features and common questions

Two ways to focus

Classic Pomodoro counts down from a fixed interval. Reverse Pomodoro is a count-up focus mode that lets you work freely, then calculates a break from how long you worked.

Key features

  • Customizable intervals, sounds, and themes
  • Daily focus streaks and productivity insights
  • Achievements and a global leaderboard
  • Installable PWA with offline timer access
  • Optional cross-device account sync

Frequently asked questions

Is Customodoro free?
Yes. No account is required to start, and Customodoro is open source under the MIT license.
What is Reverse Pomodoro?
Also called Flowmodoro, it counts up while you focus and calculates your break from how long you worked.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Install Customodoro as a PWA and the timer can run offline after the first successful load.

About Customodoro

Customodoro is a handcrafted productivity timer built to help you focus better, manage your time, and maintain balance.

Why I made this? I built this for someone special — my girlfriend — because she told me the traditional Pomodoro style wasn't working well for her study sessions. So, I designed Reverse Pomodoro just for her — something more gentle and flexible, the way she needed it.

This app is for anyone who wants to focus with intention, rest with clarity, and make every session feel like progress.
